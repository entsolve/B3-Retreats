This is a finished photograph. Retouch it so that it reads as a real snapshot instead of a staged
picture. Do not change who is in it, where they stand, what they wear or how the frame is cut.

UNCHANGED — the same three women seen from behind, faces out of frame. Same framing, same field of
view, same distance, same horizon and treeline, same relative heights with the middle woman the
tallest, same clothes (champagne satin slip dress, cream linen dress with short sleeves, brown
ribbed top with oat linen trousers), same hair colours and the same level bottom edge of the copper
curls, same arms and hands, the same hand resting on the right woman's shoulder, the same mandala
tattoo, the same field, sky, grasses and thistles, the same warm low light from behind and to the
right, the same colour grade and the same film grain.

WHAT IS WRONG NOW — everything is too even, too arranged, too clean. Three people standing equally
spaced, equally still, equally lit, with clothes that hang without a single accident. Nobody
photographs like that. Fix it with the small physical imperfections a real frame has:

1. MOVEMENT. They are walking, not posing. Give the woman on the LEFT a real mid-step: her back foot
   lifting slightly off the ground with a hint of motion blur on that foot and the hem above it,
   while the rest of the picture stays sharp. Her dress swings a little to one side because of it.
2. WIND. The air is moving. Several strands of the right woman's long hair blow across and away from
   her shoulder, a few copper curls of the middle woman lift, and the dry grasses in the foreground
   bend in the SAME direction as the hair. Not a storm — a summer evening breeze.
3. SPACING. They do not stand at equal distances. Make the gap between the left and the middle woman
   noticeably wider than the gap between the middle and the right one, and let the left woman be
   half a step further forward than she is now.
4. FABRIC. Real cloth misbehaves. The satin slip dress on the left creases where it sits on the hip
   and one thin strap has slipped a little off the shoulder. The linen dress in the middle is
   wrinkled across the back from sitting, with one deeper fold. The oat trousers on the right bunch
   slightly over the ankle.
5. THE FRAME ITSELF. The horizon is not perfectly level — tilt the whole scene by about half a
   degree. One dry stalk crosses the very bottom edge of the frame, close to the lens and therefore
   thick and soft and out of focus.

TEXTURE — keep the grain at the same strength, keep skin as skin with pores and freckles, keep the
weave of the linen and the individual stalks. Do NOT smooth, do NOT denoise, do NOT beautify. If the
result looks cleaner or glossier than the input, the retouch has failed.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN — no new people, no visible face, no hat, no new objects, no extra limb, no
missing or merged limb, no impossible hand, no text, no logo, no watermark, no crop, no zoom, no
change of the colour grade, no orange glow bath, no bloom, no lens flare, no sun star, no motion
blur anywhere except the one lifting foot and its hem.
